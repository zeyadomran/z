**Approvals and isolation:** The plan makes the essential distinction correctly. Individual approval, a working directory, a Job Object and filename filtering do not create filesystem or network isolation. Host commands may execute repository-controlled code and access user-readable files. The stated host-privilege disclosure and separate future sandbox gate are sufficient for this supervised MVP. Do not add a classifier, container platform, or sandbox implementation to resolve the findings above.

**Durability and unknown effects:** Intent-before-effect, transactional lifecycle events, result reuse, no automatic replay of unknown commands, artifact durability before result completion, and fresh approval after restart are good foundations. They correctly avoid an exactly-once claim. Recovery should retain the distinction between externally uncertain work and work proven never to have started. SOL-02 and SOL-06 complete missing boundaries around those principles; they do not require replacing SQLite or adopting an event-sourcing framework.

**Context management:** The proposed complete-cycle boundary, preserved originals, checkpoint-before-switch, valid clean-history fallback, output handles and deferred vector memory are appropriately scoped. During implementation, enforce a total request budget across instructions, schemas, assistant output and the entire next tool-result set; individual byte caps alone do not establish that budget. If even the minimum valid cycle or summary cannot fit, return the planned context-limit outcome. State the priority for pinned user constraints versus summarized history. The existing constrained-context acceptance flow is the right owner; an elaborate summary-quality benchmark is unnecessary for v0.1.

**Testing strategy:** I applied the required minimal-functional-tests skill. Section 11 substantially follows it: public engine behavior, real temporary SQLite storage, controlled external provider/process boundaries, one canonical success flow, distinct consequential failures and bounded manual platform qualification. Preserve that approach. The acceptance corrections above should extend their existing owning flows as each capability lands. Do not turn this review into a batch of speculative tests, a mirrored CLI/engine suite, a coverage-percentage gate, or a platform stress harness. Native qualification and live-model evaluations remain necessary and cannot be replaced by the scripted provider.

**Scope and ordering:** The release is substantial but coherent. No credible calendar estimate can be inferred from the plan alone. After SOL-04's prerequisite placement is corrected, the vertical progression is sensible. The name, second provider, Linux release, PTY/TUI, MCP, ACP, subagents, semantic memory, hardened sandbox and desktop application remain deferrable. Linux may remain explicitly unsupported in v0.1. Windows process lifecycle, valid resumed provider history, and cancellation independent of UI progress cannot be deferred while claiming the first release's stated behavior.

## Recommended disposition

Revise the plan to close SOL-01 through SOL-03 before their affected implementation starts, incorporate the bounded M2/M3 corrections in SOL-04 through SOL-07, and proceed with the two-crate scaffold. An independent re-review is only needed if the author wants the revised contracts independently approved; this review does not approve unseen revisions or validate a future implementation.
